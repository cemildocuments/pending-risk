# Dataset provenance and attribution

The original Python software is MIT licensed. Source datasets retain their own licenses and attribution requirements. Prediction traces are derived research outputs linked to their source records through `source_index`.

## Adult

Barry Becker and Ronny Kohavi (1996). *Adult*. UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5XW20. License stated by the repository: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Bank Marketing

Sergio Moro, Paulo Rita, and Paulo Cortez (2014). *Bank Marketing*. UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5K306. License stated by the repository: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Application reference: S. Moro, P. Cortez, and P. Rita, *A data-driven approach to predict the success of bank telemarketing*, Decision Support Systems 62 (2014), 22–31, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dss.2014.03.001.

## Gas Sensor Array Drift

Alexander Vergara (2012). *Gas Sensor Array Drift Dataset*. UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5RP6W. License stated by the repository: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Application reference: A. Vergara and colleagues, *Chemical gas sensor drift compensation using classifier ensembles*, Sensors and Actuators B: Chemical 166–167 (2012), 320–329, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.snb.2012.01.074.

## Electricity

Electricity pricing data from the Australian New South Wales Electricity Market, using the source archive documented by the River Elec2 loader: https://riverml.xyz/dev/api/datasets/Elec2/. The loader identifies the public archive at https://maxhalford.github.io/files/datasets/electricity.zip and cites M. Harries, *SPLICE-2 Comparative Evaluation: Electricity Pricing* (1999). Original dataset rights remain with the source provider. The software MIT license applies to the original experiment code, and does not relicense upstream source records.

## Transformations

The preparation script partitions the source records, removes Bank call duration, fits preprocessing only on fitting records, and generates the model inputs. Frozen traces record predictor outputs, losses, original row indices, and calibration quantities. Audit selections, delay schedules, and synthetic losses are generated by the documented seeded experimental procedures. Archive hashes and byte counts are recorded in `data/sources.json`.
